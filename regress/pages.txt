# The pages regress/compare.pl renders with the base and with a pull
# request. Each line is the part of a page's URL after cgi-bin/. A line with
# $NAME in it stands for one page per value of the list NAME, and {a,b} for
# one page per value.
#
# The pages are chosen to show as much as possible in few of them:
# 1. The calendar: the Ordo of a whole year shows 365 days of precedence,
#    commemorations, colours and notes on one page. The years are those that
#    matter to each version: the earliest and latest Easter, and the fewest
#    years that show every transfer of its tables and every Sunday letter.
# 2. The hours and the Mass: for each version, the fewest days that show
#    every kind of each hour and of the Mass at least once: where each part
#    comes from, the patterns of the lessons, the commemorations, the
#    concurrence of Vespers, the Mass's parts and preface. The days were
#    found with the Ordo of those nine years (see regress/README.md). The
#    hours of a day are one page (command=prayOmnes); Vespers is also a page
#    of its own, for the heading of its concurrence.
#    And Vespers of every day from Christmas to the octave day of the Epiphany.
# 3. The rest of the site, once each: the languages, the votive offices,
#    the dioceses, the options, and the other scripts.
